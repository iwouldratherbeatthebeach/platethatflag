About Flag That Plate

Flag That Plate was created out of interest in diplomatic license plates and the countries and mission roles they represent. It is an independent reference and observation project intended for educational and informational use.

The site logs lookup activity to improve the plate database over time. Unknown codes may be reviewed and added as new plate assignments are observed.
